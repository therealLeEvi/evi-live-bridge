EVI Live setup

Everything here runs on this computer. Nothing on this page sends your trades anywhere.

Bring over the history you already have

EVI gives better suggestions once it knows what you have traded before, and it does not need to watch you for weeks to learn that. If you have run the Exchange Logger plugin, its logs are already on this machine and EVI can read them.

Your own history is an extra safety layer, never a requirement. EVI ranks trades from the market on its own, and it does not hold back its better picks until you import anything.

Nothing has been saved yet. Importing the same log twice adds nothing: EVI recognises a trade it has already seen.

Or bring a CSV from any tracker

If you keep a log somewhere else, export it as CSV and hand it over. EVI reads the file here in your browser, works out which column is which from its heading, and shows you what it found before anything is saved.

It needs, at minimum, an item name, a quantity, what you paid, the profit after tax, and the buy and sell times. Names like Item, Quantity, Profit, Buy price, First buy time and Last sell time are all recognised. Nothing about the file leaves this computer.

Let EVI measure the market over time

EVI keeps its own record of prices as the hours pass, on this computer only. It uses it to answer two questions no single price can: can this price even be bought again, and is anyone actually paying it. Those two checks are how EVI avoids offering you a trade that cannot be finished.

On by default, because without it those checks have nothing to read and simply stay quiet. It fetches public price data from the OSRS Wiki about thirteen times an hour and writes it into your data folder. It sends nothing about you, and you can switch it off here. The first few minutes after a fresh start it collects four days of past hours, one request every 2.5 seconds, and then settles down.

Pairing RuneLite

The plugin needs the RuneLite plugin key, which is printed in the bridge's own window when it starts - not the scanner key you used to open this page. Paste it into EVI's sidebar in the client.

The plugin only ever talks to 127.0.0.1. Your suggestions are computed here, on this machine.
